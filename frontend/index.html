FAQ

Common Questions

Is my data private? +

Yes. Your queries are encrypted in transit and stored securely on Pakistan-based infrastructure. We never sell or share your data with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I cancel anytime? +

Yes. Cancel from your account page or email us at support@asktax.pk. No automatic renewals — we contact you before each billing period. See our Refund Policy.

How is this different from searching FBR's website? +

AskTax reads and understands 4,000+ FBR documents — SROs, circulars, income tax ordinances, customs rules, and case law — so you don't have to. Ask in plain English or Urdu and get a direct answer with citations linking to the original document. FBR's own website requires you to know what you're looking for.

Is this legal advice? +

No. AskTax.pk is a research assistant, not a lawyer or chartered accountant. Every answer cites its source so you can verify. Always confirm critical decisions with a qualified CA or FBR-registered tax advisor.

What payment methods do you accept? +

JazzCash, EasyPaisa, Meezan Bank transfer, and standard bank transfer. Subscription is activated manually within 1 business day of payment confirmation.
