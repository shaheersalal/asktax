Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025 · Effective for all AskTax.pk users

AskTax.pk is operated by Shaheer Salal (sole proprietor). Your data is protected and never sold. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. What We Collect

  • Account data: Name, email address, hashed password (we never store plain-text passwords).
  • Usage data: Questions you ask, AI responses generated, timestamps, and query counts.
  • Technical data: IP address (for rate-limiting and abuse prevention), browser type via standard HTTP headers.
  • Payment data: Payment amount, method, and date — recorded manually by admin after receiving confirmation. We do not store your card numbers or bank account details.
  • Profile picture: If you upload one, stored in our secure MinIO object storage.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide AI-powered tax answers from FBR documents.
  • To save your chat history so you can revisit past questions.
  • To enforce monthly query limits by subscription tier.
  • To send account-related communications (e.g. password reset if implemented).
  • To improve the accuracy of our AI model and document database.
  • To detect and prevent abuse, spam, or unauthorised access.

3. Payment Processing (Meezan Bank)

Payments are processed via Meezan Bank payment gateway or direct bank transfer. When you make a payment:

  • Your bank/card details are processed by Meezan Bank — AskTax.pk does not receive or store your card number, CVV, or PIN.
  • Meezan Bank is governed by the State Bank of Pakistan's data protection regulations and their own privacy policy.
  • We receive only a payment confirmation and transaction reference to activate your subscription.
  • If you pay via bank transfer, provide only the last 4 digits of your account for verification.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • OpenAI: Your question text is sent to OpenAI's API to generate answers. OpenAI's data processing policy applies. OpenAI does not use API inputs to train models by default.
  • Meezan Bank: Payment details are shared with Meezan Bank solely to process transactions.
  • Legal requirement: If required by Pakistani law or a valid court order.

5. Data Retention

  • Chat history is retained indefinitely to allow you to recover accidentally deleted conversations.
  • If you delete your account, your data is soft-deleted and will be permanently purged within 90 days unless you request earlier deletion.
  • IP rate-limit data is stored for 30 days.

6. Security

  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+).
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt — we cannot view your password.
  • JWT tokens expire after 30 days.
  • Our infrastructure runs in Docker containers with network isolation.

7. Cookies & Local Storage

AskTax.pk uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your login token and preferences (e.g. dark/light theme). No cross-site tracking cookies are used.

8. Your Rights

  • Access: View your data via your account page.
  • Correction: Edit your name and profile picture at any time.
  • Deletion: Request account deletion from your account page or by emailing us.
  • Data export: Email us to request a copy of your data.

9. Contact

For any privacy concerns, email: support@asktax.pk

Or use the in-app messaging on your account page (Mon–Sat, 10am–6pm PKT).
