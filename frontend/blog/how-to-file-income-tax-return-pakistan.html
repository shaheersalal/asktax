How to File Income Tax Return in Pakistan 2025

Updated: May 2025  ·  Tax Year 2024-25  ·  FBR IRIS Portal

Filing your income tax return in Pakistan is mandatory if your annual income exceeds Rs 600,000, or if you own immovable property, a vehicle, or a foreign asset — regardless of income level. Filing also puts you on the Active Taxpayer List (ATL), which saves you significant withholding tax on every transaction.

⚠️ Disclaimer: This is a general guide based on standard FBR IRIS procedures. Verify current requirements at iris.fbr.gov.pk.

Before You Start — Documents to Gather

  • CNIC — your 13-digit national identity number
  • Salary certificate (Form 16) — from your employer showing gross salary and tax deducted
  • Bank statements — for all accounts, July 2024 – June 2025
  • Property documents — if you own immovable property
  • Vehicle registration — if you own a vehicle
  • Investment certificates — National Savings, mutual funds, shares
  • Rental income records — if you receive rent
  • Foreign asset details — if applicable

Step-by-Step Filing Guide

1

Register on FBR IRIS (first-time filers only)

Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk and click "Registration." Enter your CNIC. You will receive your NTN (National Tax Number) and a temporary password via email/SMS. Verify your email to activate the account.

2

Log into IRIS

Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk, enter your CNIC and password, and complete the OTP verification. Change your password on first login.

3

Select the correct return form

From the dashboard, go to Declaration → Income Tax Return. Choose the tax year (2025 for FY 2024-25). Select "Return of Income for Salary Person" if salary exceeds 75% of income, otherwise use "Business Income Return."

4

Fill in income details

Enter gross salary from your salary certificate. Add any other income (rental, profit on debt, capital gains). The system auto-calculates tax based on slabs. Enter tax already deducted at source (from salary certificate or withholding certificates).

5

Complete the Wealth Statement

This is mandatory. Declare all assets (property, vehicles, cash, investments, foreign assets) and all liabilities (loans) as of June 30, 2025. The system checks that your net assets are consistent with your declared income. Unexplained wealth is a red flag for audit.

6

Pay any tax due

If tax payable exceeds tax already deducted, you must pay the balance before filing. Use the PSID (payment slip) generated in IRIS and pay via any bank branch, online banking, or Easypaisa/JazzCash.

7

Submit and save the acknowledgement

Click Verify and Submit. You will receive an acknowledgement number. Save or print the acknowledgement — it is your proof of filing. Your name will appear on the ATL within days of the next ATL update (every Sunday).

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Filing with incorrect CNIC or NTN — causes the return to be rejected
  • Not declaring all bank accounts — FBR receives bank data from NADRA/SBP
  • Forgetting to file the Wealth Statement — it's a separate form, not part of the income return
  • Declaring lower property value than FBR valuation tables — triggers automatic notices
  • Missing the September 30 deadline — you lose ATL status for the entire following year
⏰ Deadline reminder: For tax year 2024-25, the filing deadline for salaried individuals and AOPs is September 30, 2025. Companies must file by December 31, 2025.
