Filer vs Non-Filer in Pakistan 2025

Updated: May 2025  ·  Tax Year 2024-25  ·  Based on Finance Act 2024

In Pakistan, whether you appear on FBR's Active Taxpayer List (ATL) as a "filer" determines the withholding tax rate you pay on almost every major financial transaction — property, vehicles, banking, dividends, and more. The difference is often 2x. This page shows exactly what non-filers overpay and how to fix it in minutes.

What is a Filer?

A "filer" is anyone whose name appears on FBR's Active Taxpayer List (ATL). The ATL is updated every Sunday. To appear on it, you must have filed your income tax return for the previous tax year before September 30. That's it. If you filed, you're a filer. If you didn't, you're a non-filer — and you pay more on everything below.

Real-World Example — Property Purchase

You buy a house for Rs 20 million.

  • As a filer: withholding tax = Rs 20M × 3% = Rs 600,000
  • As a non-filer: withholding tax = Rs 20M × 6% = Rs 1,200,000
  • Penalty for not filing: Rs 600,000 extra on a single property transaction.

This is above and beyond stamp duty, CVT, and agent fees. Filing your return is the single cheapest tax-reduction action available to every Pakistani.

How to Become a Filer — 3 Steps

  1. Register on FBR IRIS at iris.fbr.gov.pk using your CNIC. You'll get an NTN immediately.
  2. File your income tax return for the last tax year. For salaried employees it takes 20–30 minutes with a salary certificate.
  3. Check ATL status after the next Sunday update. SMS "ATL [space] CNIC" to 9966, or check online at fbr.gov.pk.

Common Questions

Do I have to pay tax to become a filer? Not necessarily. If your income is below Rs 600,000, you file a nil return — zero tax payable — and still appear on ATL as a filer.

Can I become a filer for past years? Yes, you can file returns for up to 5 prior tax years. This can also make you eligible for refunds of excess withholding tax deducted in those years.

What if I missed this year's deadline? You can still file a late return but will pay a penalty under Section 182. You will appear on ATL only from the next ATL cycle after filing.
