Pakistan Income Tax Rates 2025

Updated: May 2025  ·  Tax Year 2024-25  ·  Based on Finance Act 2024

This page lists all income tax slab rates applicable in Pakistan for tax year 2024-25 (July 2024 – June 2025), covering salaried persons, business individuals, AOPs, and companies. Rates are based on the Finance Act 2024.

⚠️ Tax rates change every Finance Act. Always verify with fbr.gov.pk or a qualified tax advisor before making decisions.

1. Salaried Persons — Tax Slabs 2024-25

These rates apply to individuals whose salary income is more than 75% of their total income.
