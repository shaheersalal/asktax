Refund & Payment Policy

Last updated: May 2025 · Effective for all paid subscriptions

AskTax.pk offers a 7-day refund window for new subscribers. We want you to be confident before you commit. Read below for full details.

1. Payment Methods

We accept payments via:

  • Meezan Bank Transfer (direct bank account)
  • Meezan Bank Payment Gateway (debit/credit card via JazzCash/EasyPaisa integration)
  • Raast (Instant Payment System)
  • Manual bank transfer — send screenshot via the in-app messaging

Subscription is activated manually within 1 business day after payment confirmation.

3. Refund Policy

When We Refund

  • ✓ Within 7 days of your first payment, if you have used fewer than 20 queries.
  • ✓ Technical failure that prevented you from using the service for more than 3 consecutive days (verified by our logs).
  • ✓ Duplicate payment charged due to our system error.

When We Do Not Refund

  • ✗ After 7 days from the initial payment date.
  • ✗ If more than 20 queries have been used in the current billing period.
  • ✗ Partial months — cancellation does not trigger a prorated refund for the remaining days.
  • ✗ Annual plans after 14 days of activation (2-week cooling-off period applies instead).
  • ✗ Dissatisfaction with AI accuracy — our AI is powered by official FBR documents but does not replace a chartered accountant. Please use the free trial before subscribing.

4. Cancellation

To cancel your subscription:

  • Email support@asktax.pk or use the in-app messaging.
  • Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
  • No automatic renewals — we contact you before each renewal for manual re-payment.

5. Refund Process (Meezan Bank)

Refunds are issued to the same account or card used for payment:

  • Bank transfer refunds: processed within 2-3 business days.
  • Card refunds via Meezan Bank gateway: processed within 5-7 business days (bank processing time applies).
  • You will receive a confirmation via email or in-app message once the refund is initiated.

6. Disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact us within 30 days of the transaction. We will investigate and respond within 2 business days.

For unresolved disputes, you may contact the State Bank of Pakistan Consumer Protection Unit or your bank's dispute resolution desk.
